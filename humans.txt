## We're humans, living in a city somewhere near here

The THUxk team, Tsinghua University.

## We're trying to build a better city for everyone

Our mission is to provide a clean, organized, and reliable resource hub for Tsinghua students.
- Thubook: A survival guide for navigating the campus
- Thucourse: A community for course selection, ratings, and reviews

## We use these technologies to build the city we live in

- HTML5 / CSS3 / JavaScript
- Cloudflare Pages (hosting & CDN)
- VuePress (for Thubook documentation)
- Python (for data processing and indexing)

## This city's manual (or rulebook)

- robots.txt — tells search engines how to crawl our site
- sitemap.xml — lists all our important pages
- CONTRIBUTING.md — guidelines for contributors

## Meet the community that keeps the city running

## We work hard to keep this city running smoothly

Our site is updated regularly with fresh content, improved navigation,
and enhanced features to serve the community better.
